# Doser — Support

Doser is a log for your medical cannabis prescriptions and sessions, for iOS.

## Contact us

Email **[support@doserapp.com](mailto:support@doserapp.com)**. We aim to reply within two
working days. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Profile, at the
bottom), and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Doser can't give medical advice. For questions about your prescription, dose or side
effects, ask your prescribing clinic or pharmacist.

## Common questions

### I didn't get the confirmation or password-reset email

Check your spam or junk folder, and make sure you typed the address you signed up with. On
the "Check your email" screen and on Sign in you can ask for the confirmation email again.
To reset your password, tap **Forgot password?** on the Sign in screen.

### How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Sign in, open **Profile** and tap **Restore purchases**. Use the same Apple Account you
subscribed with.

### How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. Open **Profile → Manage Plan**, or go to
**Settings → your name → Subscriptions** on your iPhone. Cancel at least 24 hours before
the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Refunds are handled by Apple at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### How do I delete my account and data?

Open **Profile** and tap **Delete Account**. Your account and everything logged in it
(prescriptions, devices, sessions and preferences) are deleted straight away. Deleting the
app alone does not delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple
subscription, so cancel that first.
